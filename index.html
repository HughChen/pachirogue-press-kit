Elevator pitch

A papercraft pachinko roguelite where you aim physics shots into a climbing horde — Vampire Survivors pressure, Peggle-style cascades, cardboard toy-box charm.

Description

PachiRogue is a physics-based horde-survival roguelite played on a handmade toy-box board. You don’t move a character — you load balls from your hopper, aim, and fire while themed armies climb the playfield in turns. When your throws are spent, the horde steps up. Land cascading hits, draft relics, and survive the path to the boss.

The look is deliberately tactile: assets are crafted from real cardboard, construction paper, and felt, then photographed into a tabletop aesthetic. Every throw is a deliberate read — limited shots, visible loadout, and physics that pay off skillful aim with screen-clearing cascades.

Key features

  • Deliberate turns — limited throws per turn; load the right ball for the wave.
  • Physics cascades — aim and read beat reflex spam; one shot can clear a cluster.
  • Hopper loadouts — distinct ball types as physics tools (mass, bounce, damage, size).
  • Papercraft world — cardboard boards, felt enemies, mason-jar gold bank.
  • Authored levels — themed armies and boss finales across a roster of stages.
